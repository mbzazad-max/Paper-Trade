ETH ↔ BTC link

ETH (COINBASE:ETHUSD) moves with BTC (BINANCE:BTCUSDT). Correlation and beta use log returns of matching closed candles. Beta 1.2 means ETH tends to move 1.2% for every 1% BTC moves. BTC-implied ETH maps a BTC price onto ETH with that beta, so you can check one coin's level against the other.

RSI sync: of the past times BTC's RSI(7) closed below 30 (or above 70), how often ETH did the same within the window shown, and the median lag in candles (negative means ETH got there first). Weekly candles are built from daily bars. Not financial advice.

Tentative cycle

Plain RSI(7): a close below 30, then the first close above 70, then back below 30. No fib or EMA filter, so this is a timing guide, not a trade signal. Early, likely and late are the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles of past legs that had already lasted as long as this one.

Dates are candle closes in your time zone. Weekly candles are built from the daily bars (weeks start Monday 00:00 UTC). Odds are the share of those past legs that finished within that time. History: 4H since mid-2024, 1D and 1W since late 2020. Updates every 30 seconds and on each refresh. Not financial advice.
