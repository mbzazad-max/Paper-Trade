How the zones are built, and how to refresh

Levels and statuses are from the data timestamp above. Prices move; re-check a zone against the live chart before acting. This is analysis, not financial advice.

Refresh prices: the button at the top pulls live prices and RSI from your TradingView connector and updates distances and status against the saved zones. Refresh all from TradingView: pulls fresh 4H and 1D data for every coin and the market gauges, rebuilds zones, plans and statuses inside this page (about 2 to 3 minutes, keep the page open) and saves the result for the next visit. Coins that fail keep their previous data and are listed; "Fetch missing" retries only those. Only the data file changes.
