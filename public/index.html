How these paper accounts work

Fresh start. Both accounts began empty at the start time shown above. No trades, advice or results were carried over from Coin Zones or anywhere else.

Same rules as Coin Zones. Tab 01 replays the board's own spot-setup advice with the board's own paper-test rules, buying only when a 4H market structure shift up comes from the buy zone. Tab 02 is the s083 rule from the board's Strategy test. Everything uses closed 4H candles from the shared Market-Data store and is rebuilt when new candles arrive, once a day, or when you press Refresh.

Your buttons (owner only). Close all sells what is left of a position. Book % sells that share of what is left and needs it to be in profit after fees. Both fill at the close of the last closed 4H candle, never at a live price, and change only that tab. Each request is checked again by the build; if a new candle has closed or the position changed, it is refused and nothing happens.

MSS-RVOL: Separate five-coin paper test. Native closed 4H, 2H/3H derived from saved closed 1H, volume-confirmed entries and structure exits, protective ATR stop, separate persistent ledger and owner-only manual actions. No historic positions are imported.

RSI Signal Desk: buy and sell signals only (no paper account). BTC on 4H and 1D with the original Signal Desk rules and engine, from closed Binance candles in Market-Data. Its trade history is a backtest over the loaded candles.

Cycle DCA: spot account for BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, DOGE, LINK, XLM and ARB. The build buys from the BTC & ETH dashboard's cycle-tab early bottom signs ($5,000 when 2 of the 6 have fired after a 30-day low, another $5,000 when all 4 price-action signs have fired); you sell with Sell % or Close all, at the last closed 4H close. Fresh start, nothing carried over.
